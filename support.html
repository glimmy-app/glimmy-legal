什麼是 Glimmy?

Glimmy 讓你把「照片 + 便利貼」留在真實世界的地點。只有實際走到附近的人,才能解鎖看見你留下的 glimmy。

怎麼發一則 glimmy?

點右下角那顆便利貼形狀的按鈕 → 拍一張照片 → 寫下你的便利貼、拖曳放到照片上 → 選擇可見範圍 → 貼上。它會釘在你目前所在的座標。

為什麼我看不到附近的 glimmy?

陌生人的公開 glimmy,只有在你實際走到約 120 公尺內才會出現在地圖上。請確認已允許定位權限。你自己、Glimmors(好友)與群組的 glimmy 則不限距離,隨時都看得到。

怎麼加 Glimmors(好友)?

在好友頁用對方的公開 ID(GLM-XXXXXX)送出邀請;對方接受後即成為 Glimmors,你們之間的 glimmy 互相不限距離可見。

Glim-Group(群組)怎麼運作?

在群組頁建立一個 Glim-Group 並邀請成員。分享給群組的 glimmy 只有接受邀請的成員看得到,而且不限距離。任何成員都能邀請新成員;群組擁有者可以解散群組或移除成員,成員也可以隨時退出。

地點提醒沒有跳通知?

背景地點提醒需要「永遠」定位權限。請到「設定 → Glimmy → 位置」改為「永遠」。提醒只在你「走進」某張 glimmy 的範圍(跨越邊界)時觸發——若你早已站在原地不會觸發,離開再回來即可。

怎麼檢舉或封鎖?

長按任一則 glimmy 或回聲即可檢舉;在使用者個人檔案的 ⋯ 選單可檢舉或封鎖該使用者。我們會在收到檢舉後 24 小時內處理。

怎麼刪除帳號?

在「設定 → 刪除帳號」,依指示輸入你的公開 ID 確認後,即可刪除你的帳號與所有相關內容。

glimmy 多久會消失?

每則 glimmy 存在 3 天(72 小時),之後會自動移入你的私人抽屜。

What is Glimmy?

Glimmy lets you leave a photo with a sticky note at a real-world location. Only people who physically come near it can unlock and see the glimmy you left.

How do I post a glimmy?

Tap the sticky-note button in the bottom-right corner → take a photo → write your sticky note and drag it onto the photo → choose who can see it → post. It's pinned to your current coordinates.

Why can't I see nearby glimmies?

A stranger's public glimmy only appears on the map once you are physically within ~120 m of it. Please make sure location access is granted. Your own glimmies, and those from your Glimmors (friends) and groups, are visible from any distance.

How do I add Glimmors (friends)?

Send an invite using the other person's public ID (GLM-XXXXXX) on the friends screen. Once they accept, you become Glimmors and each other's glimmies are visible from any distance.

How do Glim-Groups work?

Create a Glim-Group on the groups screen and invite members. Glimmies shared with a group are visible only to accepted members, from any distance. Any member can invite others; the group owner can disband the group or remove members, and members can leave at any time.

My location reminders aren't firing.

Background location reminders require "Always" location access. Go to Settings → Glimmy → Location and set it to "Always". A reminder only fires when you walk into a glimmy's range (cross its boundary) — if you're already standing inside it, leave and return.

How do I report or block?

Long-press any glimmy or echo to report it; use the ⋯ menu on a user's profile to report or block them. We act on reports within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm by entering your public ID. This deletes your account and all associated content.

How long does a glimmy last?

Each glimmy lasts 3 days (72 hours), after which it moves to your private archive.
